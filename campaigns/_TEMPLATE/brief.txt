HOW TO ADD A CAMPAIGN
1. Copy this whole _TEMPLATE folder and rename the copy to the campaign name,
   e.g.  campaigns/Clix_Pool   (folders starting with _ are ignored)
2. Replace this text with the campaign's FULL brief: copy everything from the
   Whop/Vyro campaign page (rules, requirements, links, hashtags) and paste it here.
   You can also save it as brief.pdf or brief.docx instead.
3. Optional materials:
   materials/logo.png    - a logo the campaign wants on every clip
   materials/footage/    - videos the campaign gives you to clip (trailers, gameplay)
   materials/examples/   - example clips they like (AutoClipper matches their length)
4. Easier: use the app (Campaigns tab). It shows what AutoClipper understood and writes
   campaign.yaml, which you can open in Notepad and correct.
