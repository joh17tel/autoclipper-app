Get started

  1. Go to Campaigns → + New campaign and paste the campaign's full brief from Whop/Vyro.
  2. Check what AutoClipper understood, and fix anything.
  3. Press ▶ Start. Leave the PC on. Clips appear in Clips.

What gets more views (your posts)

1.30× = about 30% more views than your average clip. Small samples are kept close to 1.00× until there's enough data.
