Get started

  1. Go to Campaigns → + New campaign and paste the campaign's full brief from Whop/Vyro.
  2. Check what AutoClipper understood, and fix anything.
  3. Press ▶ Start. Leave the PC on. Clips appear in Clips.

Live activity

Clips

Download → copy caption → post → tick Posted. Delete the duds.

Campaigns

One campaign = one brief. Every clip follows its campaign's rules.

Select a campaign, or add a new one.

Test a video

Clip any gaming video on your PC with a campaign's rules. Good for checking quality before going live.

Progress shows in Dashboard → Live activity. Results appear in Clips.
